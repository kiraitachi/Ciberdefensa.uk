Video2Player for Android: Open Web Videos in VLC from Firefox

What it is

Samsung Internet has a small button that hands a web video to an external player. Firefox for Android does not. Video2Player is a Firefox extension (Manifest V2, Android-only) that detects videos on any page and shows a pill button under the player. One tap sends the real video URL to your default Android video player — usually VLC.

What it does

  • Finds <video> elements, including ones added by infinite scroll or late-attaching players.
  • Shows a pill button styled to match Firefox, visible after entrance animation.
  • Works out the real playable URL (not just the blob reference) and sends it via Android intent.
  • If nothing opens, shows a fallback dialog with explicit VLC / MX Player buttons and a Copy Video URL option.

Impact

Useful for anyone who prefers external players for streaming, avoids MSE/HLS playback, or wants a one-tap workflow from mobile Firefox. The extension relies on the default Android handler, so you must have a video player set as default (VLC is recommended).

How it works

The extension runs at document_idle. It watches for video elements with a throttled MutationObserver, ignores anything under 100px (tracking pixels), and tries to recover real URLs from MSE/HLS sources. It tries site-specific extractions first, then a generic regex scan over inline scripts. URLs are re-resolved at tap time, not when the button is created, since many players only settle the source after playback starts.

The intent uses the canonical form (intent://...#Intent;action=android.intent.action.VIEW;...) without pinning a package, so Android picks your default. Only the fallback dialog pins a specific app.

Limitations

  • Streaming and blob URLs. Many modern sites use MSE/HLS; the extension tries site-specific and generic extraction, but it is a best-effort heuristic and will not work everywhere.
  • DRM, encrypted media (EME), or heavily obfuscated players may not yield a usable URL.
  • Not a downloader. It passes a URL to another app; it does not fetch or store video.
  • Top frame only (for now). Iframe players are not handled in version 1.1.0.
