What I actually do

focus areas

I'm Adel — I run a cybersecurity practice out of ciberdefensa.uk, working across red teaming, penetration testing, and incident response for clients who'd rather find their weak points before someone else does.

Outside client work, I'm usually neck-deep in a Linux box somewhere, provisioning cloud infrastructure, or fixing something I broke the night before. This site is where the smaller, more personal version of that work lives.
